What should be observed?

  • Readers-Writers: readers can overlap, but writers need exclusive access to the shared value.
  • Dining Philosophers: a philosopher must acquire both neighboring chopsticks before eating.
  • Semaphore: resource access is controlled through acquire and release operations.
  • Monitor: shared state and conditions determine when a thread may proceed.
